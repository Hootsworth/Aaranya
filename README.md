### 2.2 Typography Hierarchy
- **Primary Display & Headings:**
  - Font Families: *Cormorant Garamond*, *Playfair Display*, *Newsreader* (Serif).
  - Characteristics: High contrast, elegant ascenders, lyrical italic cuts for emotional statements and institutional quotes.
  - Weight: SemiBold (600) to Regular (400).
- **Body & Information Display:**
  - Font Families: *Plus Jakarta Sans*, *Inter* (Humanist Sans-Serif).
  - Characteristics: Crystal clear legibility, generous x-height, open apertures, optimized for reading course catalogs and curriculum outlines.
  - Line Height: 1.65 to 1.8 for a relaxed, premium editorial cadence.
- **Eyebrow Headers & Meta Labels:**
  - Style: All-Caps (`text-transform: uppercase`), wide tracking (`letter-spacing: 0.18em` to `0.25em`), font size `11px - 13px`, medium weight (500/600).

### 2.3 Graphical Motifs & UI Components
- **The Botanical Leaf Accent:** Stylized leaf motif integrated into badges, section dividers, and brand blocks.
- **Editorial Asymmetry:** Newspaper and academic journal-style multi-column grids, balanced by full-bleed photography.
- **Hairline Rules & Asterisks:** Delicate lines (`1px solid rgba(22,48,36,0.12)`) and glyphs (`✦`, `*`, `—`) framing quotes.
- **Pill Badges:** Pill-shaped category markers with soft green/sage tints for academic disciplines and campus highlights.
- **Stat Counters:** Grand numerical callouts with serif figures and sans-serif uppercase labels.
