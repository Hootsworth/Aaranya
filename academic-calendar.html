MASTER ACADEMIC CALENDAR FOR ACADEMIC YEAR 2026–2027
(Applicable to All Undergraduate, Postgraduate, Law, and Doctoral Cohorts)

In exercise of the powers conferred under Section 14(2) of the Aaranya University Act, read in conjunction with Ordinance No. IV of the University Statues, and on the recommendations of the Academic Council vide Resolution No. AC/XXIV/2026/04 dated May 14, 2026, the Competent Authority is pleased to notify the Master Academic Calendar for the Academic Year 2026–2027 as outlined hereunder:

PART I: AUTUMN (MONSOON) SEMESTER 2026
Academic Event / Schedule Dates Cohorts & Operational Remarks
Course Registration July 15–18, 2026 Mandatory online registration via Student Portal for Continuing Batches
Freshman Orientation & Induction July 20–24, 2026 First-Year Undergraduate Scholars (Class of 2030)
Commencement of Instruction July 27, 2026 Teaching classes begin across all five Collegiate Schools
Last Date for Course Add/Drop August 10, 2026 Advisory sign-off from Faculty Mentors required
Mid-Semester Summative Exams September 21–26, 2026 Continuous Assessment Cycle II (30% weightage)
Autumn Recess (Festival Break) October 15–20, 2026 Academic instructional suspension
Annual Cultural Fest (Vana-Utsav) November 6–8, 2026 University-wide participation permitted without attendance forfeit
Instruction Concludes November 27, 2026 Mandatory minimum 90 instruction days completed
Preparatory Study Recess Nov 28 – Dec 2, 2026 Faculty doubt resolution & reading recess
End-Semester Examinations December 3–19, 2026 Terminal Summative Written & Laboratory Assessments
Winter Vacation & Internships Dec 20, 2026 – Jan 10, 2027 Corporate / Field practicums for 2nd & 3rd Year Scholars
PART II: SPRING SEMESTER 2027
Academic Event / Schedule Dates Cohorts & Operational Remarks
Course Registration January 05–08, 2027 Spring term course enrollment and electives selection
Instruction Commences January 11, 2027 Regular academic classes resume across campus
Mid-Semester Summative Exams March 01–06, 2027 Mid-term evaluation cycle
Spring Break (Holi Recess) March 22–27, 2027 Hostel residents permitted leave with warden clearance
Capstone Juries & Defenses April 19–23, 2027 Senior theses, Design Show & LL.M. defenses
Instruction Concludes April 30, 2027 Closure of Spring academic teaching semester
End-Semester Examinations May 06–22, 2027 Terminal spring semester assessments
Annual Convocation June 12, 2027 Conferment of Degrees upon Graduating Classes

Note on Attendance: As per Academic Ordinance VI, a student must secure a minimum of 75% aggregate attendance in each registered course to be eligible for the End-Semester Examination. Shortfall below 75% will attract debarment from examination unless condoned by the Dean on valid medical grounds.
