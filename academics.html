National Statutory Alignment

National Education Policy (NEP 2020) & Credit Architecture

Aaranya University’s undergraduate and postgraduate curricula fully implement the UGC Curriculum and Credit Framework for Undergraduate Programmes (CCFUGP), enabling flexible multidisciplinary majors, Academic Bank of Credits (ABC) mobility, and multiple entry/exit pathways.

Four-Year Undergraduate Programme (FYUP) — Multiple Entry & Exit Model Empowering learners to pause, transition, or accelerate their academic trajectory without loss of accumulated credits.
UGC CCFUGP & ABC Integrated
Stage 1 · Year 1 (Sem 1 & 2) Undergraduate Certificate Awarded upon completing 40–44 credits in foundational core and multidisciplinary courses plus a 4-credit summer vocational/community internship. 40 + 4 Credits
Stage 2 · Year 2 (Sem 3 & 4) Undergraduate Diploma Awarded upon completing 80–88 credits in disciplinary major, minor, and skill enhancement tracks plus a 4-credit summer corporate/industrial internship. 80 + 4 Credits
Stage 3 · Year 3 (Sem 5 & 6) Bachelor’s Degree Standard 3-Year graduation milestone across chosen major and interdisciplinary minor streams upon securing 120–132 certified credits. 120–132 Credits
Stage 4 · Year 4 (Sem 7 & 8) Bachelor’s (Honours / Research) Advanced specialization with an interdisciplinary capstone or 12-credit Research Dissertation (CGPA ≥ 7.5), conferring direct eligibility for Ph.D. entry. 160–176 Credits
DSC / DSE Major Discipline

Discipline-Specific Core (DSC) and Electives (DSE) covering 50% of credit volume, ensuring theoretical rigor, algorithmic depth, and laboratory mastery.

MIN / MDC Minor & Multidisciplinary

20–24 credits in a secondary school (e.g. CS major taking Cyber Law, Cognitive Science, or FinTech) alongside introductory courses across natural and social sciences.

AEC Ability Enhancement

8 mandatory credits focusing on Modern Indian Languages (MIL), Critical English Discourse, Technical Translation, and Scientific Rhetoric.

SEC Skill Enhancement

9 credits in hands-on application: Full-Stack Web Development, Data Analytics, Legal Drafting & Moot Court Advocacy, UX Prototyping, and Financial Modeling.

IKS / VAC Indian Knowledge Systems & VAC

Foundational courses in Vedic Mathematics, Nyaya Jurisprudence, Kautilyan Statecraft, Environmental Stewardship, and Constitutional Ethics (6–8 Credits).

INT / RES Internships & Research

Compulsory summer internships with Fortune 500 corporations or judicial chambers, culminating in an 8th-semester research dissertation defense.
