Admissions & Financial Aid

Discover an admissions process designed to recognize intellectual curiosity, moral courage, and authentic human potential beyond standardized marks.

The Application Journey

Four Steps to Joining Aaranya

Our holistic evaluation process considers your personal character, original thinking, and dedication to community.

01

Submit Online Profile

Complete your digital application, including personal statement of curiosity, academic transcripts, and portfolio of creative or community work.

02

Aaranya Aptitude Assessment

Take the holistic Aaranya Aptitude & Reasoning Evaluation (or submit accepted scores: SAT/ACT, JEE, CLAT, CAT, or GATE).

03

Socratic Faculty Dialogue

Shortlisted applicants participate in a conversational personal interview with faculty members exploring passion, ethics, and world perspectives.

04

Offer & Aid Allocation

Successful candidates receive formal admission alongside a personalized financial aid and scholarship package.

Equity & Inclusion

Scholarships & Need-Based Financial Aid

No deserving scholar should be denied an Aaranya education due to economic hardship. Over 40% of our student body receives institutional support.

Merit Excellence

Chancellor's Fellowship

100% tuition waiver awarded to top 5% of matriculating students demonstrating exceptional academic distinction and original creative inquiry.

Social Impact

Aranya Green Stewardship Grant

50% to 75% tuition assistance for applicants with proven track records in grassroots ecological conservation, social justice, or rural development.

Need-Based

Need-Blind Family Bursary

Variable fee subsidies, on-campus accommodation waivers, and study stipend tailored to demonstrated household financial need.
