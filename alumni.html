The Roots Initiative

Aaranya Roots: 1-on-1 Alumni Mentorship

The journey from lecture hall to leading edge is rarely a straight path. Through the Roots Mentorship Network, every penultimate and final-year student is paired with an accomplished alumnus in their chosen domain for 6 months of structured, confidential guidance.

  • Resume & Portfolio Audits: Direct feedback from hiring managers at Fortune 500s and elite chambers.
  • Mock Technical & Case Interviews: Realistic sparring in algorithms, constitutional mooting, and strategy cases.
  • Graduate School Advisory: Comprehensive SOP reviews for Fulbright, Rhodes, and Ivy League applications.
