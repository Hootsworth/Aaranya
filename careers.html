The Aaranya Faculty Recruitment Process

How we identify, nurture, and support outstanding scholarly minds.

01

Dossier Scrutiny

Evaluation of CV, publications, teaching philosophy statement, and 3 confidential peer reference letters by the Departmental Committee.

02

Research Colloquium

Candidate delivers a 45-minute seminar presenting their current research trajectory followed by an open Q&A with faculty and graduate scholars.

03

Masterclass Demo

Conduct an interactive teaching session with undergraduate students to demonstrate pedagogical mastery, engagement, and clarity.

04

Statutory Interview

Formal interview with the Statutory Selection Committee chaired by the Vice-Chancellor, School Dean, and External Subject Experts.
