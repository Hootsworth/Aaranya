The Headquarters of Student Life

The Forum: Building #10 on the Lake

Overlooking the serene waters of Aaranya Lake, The Forum is the heartbeat of student governance and creative expression. Open 24/7, this four-storey architectural sanctuary provides dedicated club suites, soundproof band rehearsal rooms, a black-box theatre, podcast recording studios, and a vibrant central amphitheatre.

“We don't just attend college here; we build a culture we are proud of.”
Acoustic Music Studios Pro tools, drum kits, grand piano, and mixing consoles.
Makerspace & Prototyping 3D printers, electronics workbenches, and robotic jigs.
Black-Box Drama Lab Configurable stage lighting, acoustic walls, green room.
Student Council Chambers Parliamentary debate hall and club executive offices.

The 5 Collegiate Guilds

45+ Registered Student Collectives

Every student at Aaranya finds a second home among peers who share their deepest curiosities, creative expressions, and competitive drive.

Performing & Literary Arts

Celebrating heritage, stagecraft, poetry, and classical music traditions.

  • Dhwani Music Society: Classical Hindustani, Carnatic, fusion, and indie rock collectives.
  • Natya Theatre Society: Street plays, proscenium drama, and bilingual script workshops.
  • Aaranya Debating Union (ADU): Parliamentary debates, British Parliamentary circuits, and moot salons.
  • Kavya Literary Guild: Creative writing, bilingual poetry slams, and annual journal publication.
  • Nritya Dance Troupe: Classical Bharatanatyam, Kathak, and contemporary dance choreography.

Technology & Innovation

Where builders, hackers, and algorithmic thinkers craft real systems.

  • Aaranya Coding Guild: Competitive programming, ICPC prep, and open-source contributions.
  • Robotics & Automation Society: Autonomous rovers, combat robots, and industrial sensor jigs.
  • AI & Machine Learning Lab: Neural model paper reviews, Kaggle challenges, and ethical audits.
  • Makers Guild: 3D printing, laser-cut hardware, mechanical automata, and circuit design.
  • E-Cell (Entrepreneurship Hub): Seed accelerator weekends, venture incubations, and investor pitch sessions.

Design & Visual Media

Exploring visual narrative, film, interaction design, and fine arts.

  • Typography Society: Letterpress workshops, Indic font design, and zine publishing.
  • IxD / UX Guild: Usability sprints, Figma component libraries, and spatial UI research.
  • Shutterbugs Photography Club: Wildlife photography along Aaranya Lake and darkroom film printing.
  • Cinephile Film Collective: Weekly cinema screenings, screenwriting salons, and student short films.
  • Fine Arts Studio: Watercolor, pottery, oil on canvas, and sculpture exhibitions.

Sports & Outdoor Adventure

Physical vitality, teamwork, and outdoor exploration in nature.

  • The Arena Football Club: University XI competing in national inter-varsity championships.
  • Aaranya Basketball Association: Floodlit tournaments and intra-collegiate hostel leagues.
  • Cricket XI & Net Practicums: Turf pitch coaching, weekend leather-ball tournaments.
  • Nature Trails & Trekking Club: 2.5 km campus biodiversity trail walks and Western Ghats treks.
  • Lakeside Yoga & Mindfulness: Sunrise pranayama, open-air meditation, and wellness workshops.

Social Impact & Sustainability

Serving communities, protecting ecology, and promoting civil justice.

  • Green Earth Environmental Alliance: Lake cleanups, wetland conservation, and composting.
  • Vidya Rural Education Mission: Weekend STEM and English coaching in nearby village schools.
  • Vidhi Nyaya Legal Literacy Clinic: Free legal counseling and civil rights awareness camps.
  • Karuna Animal Welfare Society: Campus stray care, vaccination, and wildlife rescue support.
  • Sustainable Living Collective: Zero-waste cooking, second-hand book fairs, and ethical swaps.

Student Activity Council (SAC)

Democratically elected student leadership representing all collegiate schools.

  • Annual Budget Allotment: Independent management of ₹35L in student club grants.
  • Elections & Transparency: Direct secret-ballot elections held every autumn semester.
  • Student Welfare & Advocacy: Regular ombudsman dialogues with University executive leadership.
  • Event Clearance & Logistics: Sound permits, campus space booking, and festival coordination.
Aaranya Traditions

Annual Flagship Festivals

Marking the rhythm of the academic year with music, innovation, debate, and celebration.

Spring Semester · February

Vana-Utsav Festival

Aaranya's 3-day annual cultural extravaganza. Over 5,000 students from across the nation gather for music concerts, theatre productions, battle of bands, and culinary pop-ups by the lake.

Autumn Semester · October

HackAaranya

A 48-hour interdisciplinary national hackathon where engineers, designers, lawyers, and economists collaborate to build working software and hardware prototypes for real social challenges.

Winter Semester · November

Aaranya Model UN (AMUN)

Over 400 delegates simulate the UN General Assembly, Security Council, and specialized treaty bodies, drafting resolutions on climate mitigation, artificial intelligence, and disarmament.

Annual Showcase · April

Design Triennale & Expo

Curated by the School of Design & Innovation, showcasing student prototypes, kinetic installations, bio-materials, and publications before international design juries and corporate hiring directors.

Monthly Starlight Tradition

Lakeside Acoustic Nights

Held every full-moon Friday at the Lakeside Learning Pavilion (Landmark #12). Students gather with guitars, flutes, poetry, and tea under the starry night sky.

Annual Open · January

Aaranya Parliamentary Debate

India's premier Asian Parliamentary debating tournament, hosting collegiate teams from Oxford, NUS, St. Stephen's, NLSIU, and IITs in spirited intellectual discourse.

Start Something New

Have an idea for a new student society?

Any group of 5 enrolled students with a faculty mentor can propose a new registered club to the Student Activity Council and unlock initial seed grants of ₹50,000.
