Zero Tolerance Policy

Anti-Ragging Cell & Flying Squad

Ragging in any form is strictly prohibited across all collegiate schools, residential hostels, dining pavilions, transport buses, and sports grounds.

In full conformity with the Hon'ble Supreme Court of India directives and UGC Regulations, Aaranya University enforces an absolute zero-tolerance framework. Ragging is defined as any disorderly conduct whether by words spoken or written, or by an act that has the effect of teasing, treating, or handling with rudeness any other student.

Mandatory Undertakings

At the inception of every academic session, every enrolled student and their parent/guardian must execute an online anti-ragging affidavit through the National Anti-Ragging Web Portal (antiragging.in) and submit the verification reference number to the Registrar's Office during registration.

Statutory Penalties under Ordinance 22

  • Immediate suspension from attending lectures and academic privileges.
  • Withholding of examination results, scholarships, and fellowships.
  • Permanent expulsion from university residential hostels.
  • Rustication from the institution for periods varying from 1 to 4 semesters.
  • Mandatory registration of a First Information Report (FIR) with the local police precinct.
