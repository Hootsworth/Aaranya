Examination & Grading Ordinances — AY 2026

The institutional framework governing continuous formative evaluations, terminal examinations, 10-point CGPA calculation, academic integrity, and script re-evaluations.

Ordinance No. AU/COE/EXAM-ORD/2026-04 Enacted in compliance with UGC Choice Based Credit System (CBCS) guidelines and approved by the Academic Council for all Degree Programs.
Evaluation Framework

Assessment Weightage Architecture

Aaranya University departs from rigid rote learning by implementing a balanced continuous formative evaluation model. Every semester course is evaluated through two distinct streams:

1. Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE)

40%

Ongoing formative assessment administered throughout the semester:

  • Mid-Semester Written Examination: 15%
  • Case Studies, Lab Practicals, Studio Crits & Term Papers: 15%
  • Unannounced Conceptual Quizzes & Seminar Presentations: 10%

2. End-Semester Examination (ESE)

60%

Comprehensive summative evaluation conducted in proctored exam halls:

  • Centralized Written Theory Examination (3 Hours): 50%
  • External Viva-Voce / Practical Jury Examination: 10%
Academic Eligibility

Attendance & Hall Ticket Rules

75%

Mandatory Minimum Attendance

Per individual course unit

No student shall be allowed to appear in the End-Semester Examination of a course unless they have attended at least 75% of the total instructional sessions delivered in that course.

Medical Condonation: The Dean of the School may condone attendance shortages up to a maximum of 10% (i.e. between 65% and 74.9%) strictly on grounds of certified hospitalization or representing the University at national conferences.

Hall Ticket Issuance

Admit Cards / Hall Tickets are generated online via the Student SIS portal 7 days prior to examination commencement, subject to zero fee dues and library clearance.

Computation Methodology

SGPA & CGPA Mathematical Formula

Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA) is the weighted average of the grade points obtained in all registered courses in a term:

SGPA = Σ(Course Credit × Grade Point) / Σ(Total Course Credits)

CGPA to Percentage Conversion Formula:
As per Aaranya University Statute 12.4, official percentage equivalent is computed as:
Equivalent Percentage (%) = CGPA × 9.5

Conferment of Honors

Final Degree Classifications

  • First Class with Distinction (Honours): CGPA of 8.50 and above with zero backlog history throughout all semesters.
  • First Class: CGPA between 6.75 and 8.49.
  • Second Class: CGPA between 5.50 and 6.74.
  • Pass Class: CGPA between 5.00 and 5.49 (minimum degree completion requirement).
Academic Grievance & Recourse

Script Inspection & Re-evaluation Protocols

To preserve uncompromising transparency and student trust, the Office of the Controller of Examinations maintains a structured 3-tier post-result review mechanism:

Step 1 (Within 7 Days)

Photocopy / Digital Script Inspection

Students apply online to receive an unredacted evaluated copy of their answer booklet alongside the official scoring rubric.

Step 2 (Within 14 Days)

Independent Re-Evaluation

Script is blinded and dispatched to an external professor from a premier central institution. Revised marks supersede previous score if deviation exceeds 5%.

Step 3 (Continuous)

Supplementary Re-Exams

Special supplementary exams are held during the winter/summer breaks so scholars with backlogs can graduate on schedule without losing an academic year.

ORDINANCE GOVERNING CONDUCT OF EXAMINATIONS, CONTINUOUS ASSESSMENT, AND 10-POINT CGPA SYSTEM (AY 2026 ONWARDS)

In exercise of the powers conferred by Section 21 of the Aaranya University Charter and on the recommendation of the Board of Examinations and Academic Council, the University hereby promulgates the revised Academic Regulations on Examinations, Grading and Award of Degrees:

CLAUSE 1: CONTINUOUS FORMATIVE & SUMMATIVE ASSESSMENT RATIO

1.1 Every credit-bearing course shall be evaluated for 100 maximum marks apportioned as: Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE) = 40 Marks, and End-Semester Examination (ESE) = 60 Marks.
1.2 To pass a course, a candidate must obtain at least 40% marks in the End-Semester Examination and an aggregate of 45% (or Grade C / P as applicable) combining CIE and ESE.
