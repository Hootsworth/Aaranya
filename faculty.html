SK

Dr. Sanjeev Kulkarni

Dean & Professor of Computer Science

Ph.D., Stanford University. Specializes in scalable machine intelligence, distributed algorithms, and ethical computing architectures. Former lead researcher at Bell Labs.

Prof. Priya Venkatesh

Associate Professor, AI & HCI

Ph.D., Carnegie Mellon University. Research focuses on human-centered AI interfaces, assistive speech computation, and algorithmic bias in multilingual environments.

Professor of Systems & Security

Ph.D., IISc Bangalore. Expert in high-performance kernel design, cryptographic security protocols, and decentralized consensus networks.

School of Liberal Arts & Sciences Faculty

School Portal →
AG

Dr. Arundhati Ghosh

Dean & Professor of Philosophy

D.Phil., University of Oxford. Author of three acclaimed monographs on epistemology, classical Indian philosophy of mind, and the ethics of technology.

Office: Liberal Arts Block, Suite 401 · a.ghosh@aaranya.edu.in
VK

Prof. Vikramaditya Kapur

Professor of Environmental Sciences

Ph.D., Cambridge University. Spearheads the Aaranya Lake ecological restoration project, analyzing freshwater ecosystems and bio-retention swales.

Office: Sciences Wing, Lab 12 · v.kapur@aaranya.edu.in
TI

Dr. Tara Iyer

Associate Professor of Cognitive Science

Ph.D., Harvard University. Investigates neural linguistic processing, human decision-making under uncertainty, and cross-cultural cognition.

Office: Liberal Arts Block, Suite 210 · t.iyer@aaranya.edu.in
