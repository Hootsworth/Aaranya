Institutional Architecture

Statutory Authorities & Councils

The democratic deliberative bodies empowered under the University Charter to govern policy and academic standards.

Supreme Governing Body

Board of Governors

The paramount legislative body determining institutional policies, annual budget allocations, building plans, and statutory amendments.

  • Chaired by the Chancellor
  • Vice-Chancellor & 2 Pro-VCs
  • 3 Nominees from Sponsoring Foundation
  • 2 Eminent Jurists / Scientists
  • 1 Principal Secretary of Higher Education
Academic Standards

The Academic Council

The principal academic authority responsible for maintaining standards of instruction, curriculum vetting, examinations, and degree awards.

  • Chaired by the Vice-Chancellor
  • Deans of all 5 Collegiate Schools
  • Heads of Academic Departments
  • Registrar (Secretary Ex-Officio)
  • Controller of Examinations
Financial Oversight

The Finance Committee

Advises the Board of Governors on capital expenditures, scholarship allocations, endowment management, and external audits.

  • Chaired by the Vice-Chancellor
  • Chief Financial Officer (Secretary)
  • 2 Board of Governors Nominees
  • 1 Senior Chartered Accountant
  • Registrar (Ex-Officio)
