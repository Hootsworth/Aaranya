The Aaranya Health Centre & Inpatient Infirmary

A modern, state-of-the-art medical facility providing emergency stabilization, routine primary consultations, preventative immunizations, and round-the-clock observation.

Designed within the tranquil southern woodland sector of campus, the Aaranya Health Centre is equipped with advanced clinical diagnostic equipment, automated external defibrillators (AED), nebulizers, sterile treatment suites, and oxygen-supported observation beds.

Core Clinical Capabilities

  • 24/7 Immediate Triage: Treatment of acute illnesses, lacerations, sports fractures, allergic reactions, and seasonal infections.
  • On-Site Diagnostic Testing: Complete blood count, lipid profiles, metabolic screenings, urinalysis, and rapid viral panels.
  • Dedicated Campus Ambulance: Cardiac life-support (BLS/ACLS) ambulance on 24-hour campus standby for swift transfer to tertiary hospital partners.
  • Non-Profit University Pharmacy: Fully stocked with essential generic pharmaceuticals, first-aid supplies, and chronic ailment maintenance medications.
