Welcoming the World to Aaranya

Inbound International Students & Scholars

Each year, over 120 international exchange students, full-degree undergraduates, and visiting researchers make our 250-acre biophilic sanctuary their home.

1. Dedicated International Residences (Lotus Wing)

Climate-controlled single and twin en-suite rooms with dedicated kitchenettes, 24/7 power backup, high-speed Wi-Fi, and personalized dietary accommodation at The Commons.

2. Global Buddy & Cultural Mentor Network

Every arriving international scholar is paired with a senior Aaranya ambassador for airport pickup, city orientation, regional excursions, and language assistance.

3. Academic Credit Transfer Transparency

All coursework at Aaranya is certified with detailed syllabi, ECTS credit mapping, and prompt transcript dispatch to home university registrars upon semester conclusion.

Regulatory Compliance

Visa & FRRO Concierge Desk

The Office of International Affairs operates a dedicated government liaison desk that manages legal compliance from admission to departure:

  • Official Visa Letter: Immediate dispatch of Form 80 / Bonafide Admission Letter for Indian Student Visa (S-Visa) or Research Visa (R-Visa).
  • FRRO Online Portal Registration: Mandatory Foreigners Regional Registration Office (FRRO) assistance within 14 days of campus arrival.
  • International Comprehensive Medical Cover: Mandatory 24/7 on-campus infirmary coverage plus pan-India cashless emergency hospitalization.
  • Visa Extensions & No Objection Certificates (NOC): Handled directly through the Registrar’s legal team for scholars continuing into thesis research.
