Scholarly Journals

University Publications

Aaranya University houses three distinct student and faculty publications committed to open-access scholarly discourse:

1. The Aaranya Review

Peer-reviewed triannual journal exploring intersections between ecology, computational technology, and humanistic ethics. Indexed in DOAJ.

2. Juris Aaranya

Flagship legal periodical of the School of Law focusing on climate constitutionalism, algorithmic accountability, and human rights.

3. The Green Herald

Independent, student-run investigative campus weekly covering student governance, cultural criticism, and sustainability audits.
