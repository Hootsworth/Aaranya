Ideas to Enterprises. Enterprises to a Kinder World.

Global Perspectives. Grounded Values. Real-World Change.

At the School of Business and Economics, we combine analytical rigour with human insight. Here, you'll explore how markets work, why people make choices, and how businesses can create lasting value — for individuals, communities, and the planet.

“Better businesses for a brighter world.”

Why Business and Economics at Aaranya?

  • Real-World Relevance: Live case-studies, corporate residencies, and global immersions.
  • Interdisciplinary Perspectives: Unify business, economics, technology, and humanities.
  • Impact-Driven Leadership: Value creation extending beyond profit to ESG and equity.
  • Supportive Community: A diverse, ambitious cohort that challenges and inspires you.

B.A. in Business & Society & M.Sc. in Economics

Interdisciplinary Commerce & Advanced Postgraduate Economic Theory

Specialized & Graduate Programs

B.A. in Business & Society Core Modules:

Culture, Markets & Power

Economic Sociology, Business Anthropologies, Market Failures, and the Moral Foundations of Capitalism.

Corporate Public Policy

Regulatory Capture, Consumer Protection, Labor Rights, and State-Business Relations in Emerging Economies.

Social Impact Practicum

Year-long field immersion with social ventures, microfinance bodies, and fair-trade cooperatives.

M.Sc. in Economics Curriculum (2 Years):

Features rigorous graduate training in Advanced Microeconomic Theory (General Equilibrium & Asymmetric Information), Advanced Macroeconomics (DSGE Models & Fiscal Policy), Econometric Machine Learning, and an empirical Master's Thesis under individual faculty supervision.
