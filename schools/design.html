Our Vision

Design for People. Innovate for a Kinder Tomorrow.

At the School of Design and Innovation, we empower curious minds to reimagine the world. Through design, technology, and human-centred thinking, we create solutions that are thoughtful, inclusive, and impactful.

“Design is not just what it looks like, but what it makes possible.”

The Four Design Pillars:

  • •
    Interdisciplinary Learning: Cross-pollination with computer science labs, cognitive psychology, and ecological sciences.
  • •
    Industry Collaborations: Live briefs from leading technology companies, creative agencies, and NGOs.
  • •
    Innovation Labs: 24/7 access to physical computing, bio-materials, and digital fabrication machinery.
  • •
    Real-World Impact: Creating regenerative, accessible artifacts that enrich community wellbeing.
