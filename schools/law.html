Rooted in Principle. Driven by People.

Law for a More Human Tomorrow.

At the School of Law, we see law not just as a profession, but as a powerful tool to build a more equitable, inclusive and sustainable world. Here, you'll engage with ideas, challenge conventions, and learn to create meaningful change — in courts, communities, and beyond.

“Law thrives when more voices belong.”

Why Law at Aaranya?

  • Real-World Learning: From clinical classrooms to moot courts, arbitration centers, and grassroots legal aid.
  • Interdisciplinary Perspectives: Interweave jurisprudence with emerging technologies, climate justice, and economics.
  • Impact-Driven Opportunities: Active advocacy in constitutional civil liberties, environmental law, and public policy.
  • Supportive Community: Learn, debate, collaborate, and grow with a diverse community of legal thinkers.

Recommended Minors (20–24 Credits)

Minor in Financial Crime & Forensic Audit Offered with Business: White-collar crime detection, corporate accounting fraud, and AML compliance.
Minor in Cyber Security & Digital Evidence Offered with CS: Cloud forensics, cryptographic proofs in court, and forensic data recovery.
Minor in Public Administration & Policy Reform Offered with Liberal Arts: Bureaucratic governance, administrative law, and legislative drafting.
Minor in Bioethics & Health Jurisprudence Offered with Natural Sciences: Clinical trial regulations, genetic privacy, and public health mandates.

Specialized Elective Pools

Banking, Insolvency & Debt Recovery Laws Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC), SARFAESI Act, and corporate distress resolution.
Anti-Trust, Competition Law & Cartel Litigation Abuse of dominant position, merger control regimes, and CCI appellate procedures.
Energy, Mining & Infrastructure Project Finance Law Concession agreements, PPP contracts, and environmental tribunal compliance.
Media, Entertainment & Sovereign Speech Regulations Defamation, OTT censorship guidelines, copyright in digital broadcasting, and fair use.

LL.M. Specialization Clusters

Corporate & Commercial Law Cross-border mergers, international commercial arbitration, private equity contracts, and corporate bankruptcy.
Technology & Cyber Governance AI algorithmic liability, cross-border data transfer, platform immunity, fintech, and digital anti-trust.
Constitutional & Human Rights Comparative constitutional adjudication, socio-economic rights, international refugee law, and secularism jurisprudence.
Environmental & Climate Justice Transboundary climate litigation, biodiversity treaties, carbon tax compliance, and indigenous ecological rights.

Postgraduate Elective Pools

Transnational Mergers & Cross-Border Tax BEPS frameworks, double taxation avoidance treaties, and CFIUS-style regulatory scrutiny.
Algorithmic Liability & AI Safety Jurisprudence Autonomous agent contracts, deepfake evidentiary rules, and algorithmic discrimination.
International Trade Law & WTO Dispute Settlement Tariff schedules, subsidy challenges, appellate body crisis, and digital trade agreements.
Intellectual Property in Sovereign Genetic Resources Nagoya Protocol compliance, geographical indications, and biopiracy litigation.

Global Earth Jurisprudence & Planetary Accountability Rights of nature, intergenerational climate justice, ecocide codification, and common heritage of humanity.

Doctoral Seminars & Electives

International Investment Treaties & ISDS Arbitrations Bilateral investment treaties, regulatory expropriation, and sovereign policy autonomy.
