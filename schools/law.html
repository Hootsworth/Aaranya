Rooted in Principle. Driven by People.

Law for a More Human Tomorrow.

At the School of Law, we see law not just as a profession, but as a powerful tool to build a more equitable, inclusive and sustainable world. Here, you'll engage with ideas, challenge conventions, and learn to create meaningful change — in courts, communities, and beyond.

“Law thrives when more voices belong.”

Why Law at Aaranya?

  • Real-World Learning: From clinical classrooms to moot courts, arbitration centers, and grassroots legal aid.
  • Interdisciplinary Perspectives: Interweave jurisprudence with emerging technologies, climate justice, and economics.
  • Impact-Driven Opportunities: Active advocacy in constitutional civil liberties, environmental law, and public policy.
  • Supportive Community: Learn, debate, collaborate, and grow with a diverse community of legal thinkers.

Postgraduate & Doctoral Law (LL.M. & Ph.D.)

LL.M. Specializations · Fully Funded Doctoral Research Fellowships

Advanced Legal Scholarship

LL.M. Core Curriculum & Specialization Clusters:

Corporate & Commercial Law

Cross-Border Mergers, International Commercial Arbitration, Insolvency & Bankruptcy Code, Competition Law.

Technology & Cyber Governance

AI & Algorithmic Liability, Data Privacy Jurisprudence (GDPR & DPDPA), Fintech Law, Platform Regulation.

Constitutional & Human Rights

Comparative Constitutional Law, International Human Rights Covenants, Climate Justice Jurisprudence.

Doctoral (Ph.D.) Requirements:

Doctoral candidates undertake compulsory coursework in Advanced Legal Research Methodologies and Quantitative Empirical Analysis of Judgments, followed by oral defense of the doctoral proposal and submission of an original book-length dissertation reviewed by external national and international legal scholars.
