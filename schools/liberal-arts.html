Apply for Liberal Arts View Full Curriculum
Rooted in Curiosity. Open to the World.

More Than a Discipline. A Different Way of Seeing.

At the School of Liberal Arts and Sciences, we believe that the most meaningful progress comes from curiosity without boundaries. Here, you'll explore ideas, people, cultures, and systems — and learn to connect them, question them, and reimagine them.

“Different disciplines. A more complete you.”

The Four Core Pillars:

  • Interdisciplinary Learning: Break boundaries. Connect ideas across natural and social domains.
  • World-Ready Perspective: Understand people. Engage courageously with the global community.
  • Real-World Impact: Turn abstract ideas into public policy, civic reform, and cultural action.
  • A Supportive Community: Diverse backgrounds joined in shared dedication to truth and empathy.
